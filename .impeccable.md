## Design Context

### Users
ScoreHub is used during live badminton events by committee operators and referees who need fast, error-resistant score control on phones or laptops. A second audience is venue viewers watching read-only displays on TV or videotron, where clarity at distance is critical.

### Brand Personality
Fast, clear, and dependable. The product should feel operationally focused and confident under pressure, with minimal distraction and high legibility.

### Aesthetic Direction
Tournament utility interface with strong typographic hierarchy, condensed emphasis for scoreboard moments, and disciplined metadata styling. Prioritize clear contrast and readable controls over decorative flourishes.

### Design Principles
1. Typography must optimize match-day readability first: body at 1rem minimum, labels secondary but still legible.
2. Hierarchy should be obvious in one glance: display text for scores, strong headings for sections, restrained meta labels.
3. Keep operator actions calm and unambiguous: predictable scale, spacing, and weight across control surfaces.
4. Preserve performance and stability: lightweight font usage and minimal layout shift.
5. Treat display as read-only broadcast: distance legibility and contrast outrank stylistic novelty.
